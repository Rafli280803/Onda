The Garden Villa

A private tropical sanctuary designed for absolute seclusion and immersion in nature.

group 2 Guests
bed 1 Bedroom

restaurant Breakfast Included

Hidden beneath a canopy of ancient banyan trees, The Garden Villa offers an unparalleled retreat into nature. The architecture seamlessly blends interior luxury with the raw beauty of the surrounding jungle, utilizing locally sourced dark woods and volcanic stone to create an atmosphere of grounded elegance.

Expansive sliding glass walls open completely to a private courtyard, blurring the lines between inside and out. Here, a plunge pool finished in deep emerald tiles mirrors the lush foliage, providing a cool respite during the balmy tropical afternoons.
